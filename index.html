How it works

1

Hold a Binance .ai Agent ID in your wallet — it is your entry pass, checked by the contract on every buy.

2

Buy 1–20 tickets per transaction at exactly 0.0005 BNB each. 65% of every ticket lands in today's prize pool; 35% is the project fee.

3

The day ends at 00:00 UTC. Anyone can then call settleDay() — the contract draws one winning ticket from the day's entry ranges.

4

The winning ticket's owner receives the entire prize pool — the 65% shares of every ticket sold that day — paid by the contract in the same transaction.

§

The rules — exactly as the contract enforces them

  1. Who can play. Your wallet must hold a Binance .ai Agent ID (the SPACE ID .ai NFT at 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F). The contract checks this on every enter().
  2. Tickets. One ticket costs exactly 0.0005 BNB — a constant in the contract that nobody can change. Buy 1–20 tickets per transaction; repeat buys are allowed and your tickets add up. The draw itself is not an NFT and needs no approvals.
  3. The split. Every ticket is split at entry: 65% forms that UTC day's prize pool and 35% is the project fee, credited to the owner instantly inside the same transaction. Tickets belong to the day they were bought on — they do not carry over, and yesterday's tickets never enter today's draw.
  4. The draw. After the day ends at 00:00 UTC, anyone can call settleDay(). One ticket index is drawn from the day's range; the wallet whose entry range contains it wins the entire prize pool — the accumulated 65% shares of that day's tickets — paid automatically. Nothing more is deducted at settle: the fee was already taken at entry.
  5. How the winner is picked — honestly. The winning index is derived from a hash of the day, the day's total tickets and the hash of the block right before the settle transaction. Anyone can recompute it after the settle and verify the winner. It is transparent and tamper-evident, but it is not VRF-grade randomness: a settler chooses when to call, and timing can influence the result. Play with amounts whose loss you can shrug off.
  6. Empty days. A day with zero entries settles with no winner and no payment. If several days pass unsettled, settleDay() settles one day per call until it catches up.
  7. Owner powers are narrow. The owner can pause new entries (settling and prize pools are never affected) and withdraw only the accumulated 35% entry fees. The owner can never touch a prize pool, change the ticket price or the 65/35 split, or pick a winner.
  8. No promises. Buying a ticket buys a chance, never a guaranteed win. The only payout the contract makes is the winner's prize — the day's full prize pool — at settle. Odds are exactly your tickets divided by the day's total tickets.

Questions, answered straight

Why can only Agent ID holders play?

AgentDraw is built for the Binance Agent ID ecosystem: the .ai Agent ID is the entry pass, and the contract checks balanceOf on the registry (0x60B9…022F) on every enter(). One identity standard keeps the draw inside the agent economy it was made for — and the agent prompts, llms.txt and draw.json on this site are written for exactly those holders' agents.

What happens if nobody calls settleDay()?

Nothing is lost and nothing is stuck. The finished day's pool simply waits in the contract — settling is permissionless, so anyone (a player, an agent, you) can call settleDay() at any later time. Each call settles the oldest unsettled day, so a backlog clears one day per call until it catches up.

Can the owner touch the prize pool?

No. The only balance the owner can withdraw is the accumulated 35% entry fees, which are credited separately at entry. Prize pools sit in their own accounting until settleDay() pays them to the winner — the contract's invariant is balance == owner fees + unsettled prize pools, and it was proven in the test suite, including a withdraw attempt against a live pool.

When exactly does the day roll over?

At 00:00 UTC — 07:00 WIB. A day is the UTC day index (timestamp / 86400): tickets bought before the rollover belong to the old day, tickets after it belong to the new one. The countdown in the hero is the time left in the current day.

How is the winner picked — and can it be checked?

At settle, the contract derives the winning ticket as keccak256(day, totalTickets, blockhash of the block before the settle transaction) mod totalTickets, +1, then pays the wallet whose entry range contains that ticket. Anyone can recompute it after the fact and verify the winner — but be straight about the limit: this is verifiable-after-the-fact, not VRF-grade randomness, because the settler picks the moment of the call.

When is the 35% fee taken?

Instantly, at entry, inside the same transaction: of every 0.0005 BNB ticket, exactly 0.000175 BNB is credited to the owner's fee balance and 0.000325 BNB joins the prize pool. Nothing more is deducted at settle — the winner receives 100% of the accumulated pool. The split is a constant in the contract and cannot be changed.

What happens on a day with zero entries?

It settles cleanly: no winner, no payment, and the next settleDay() call moves on to the following day. An empty day costs nobody anything.

How many tickets can I buy?

1–20 tickets per enter() transaction, at exactly 0.0005 BNB per ticket — any other amount is rejected by the contract. There is no limit on the number of transactions: buy again as often as you like and your tickets add up in today's ranges.
